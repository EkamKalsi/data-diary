## Coverage check

Once filled in, verify your 5 stories collectively cover:

- [ ] At least 1 story per theme (partnerships, project mgmt, data, conflict)
- [ ] At least one story where you **failed** or made the wrong call initially — the recovery is what matters
- [ ] At least one story that shows **quantitative impact** in dollars, users, or percent
- [ ] At least one story that involves a **cross-functional partner** (PM, Eng, Marketing, S&O — the interviewer's lens)
- [ ] At least 8 of the 12 DD values touched across the set
