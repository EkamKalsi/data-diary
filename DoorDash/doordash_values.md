# DoorDash Values — Reference for Behavioral Prep

Twelve values, grouped four × three. Behavioral answers in the onsite should map cleanly to one or two of these. Use this as a tagging reference for the STAR story bank.

---

## WE ARE LEADERS

| # | Value | What it means in practice | Story tag idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Be an owner** | "Not my job" is never said. Pick up problems outside your formal scope. | Times you fixed something across team boundaries |
| 2 | **Dream big, start small** | Big ambitious vision, but start with a small testable bet, gather evidence, then scale. | Times you proposed something ambitious but landed it with an MVP |
| 3 | **Choose optimism, and have a plan** | Positive framing + concrete next step, especially in adversity. | Times the project was failing and you re-framed the team forward |

## WE ARE DOERS

| # | Value | What it means in practice | Story tag idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Bias for action** | Ship before perfect; analysis paralysis is the enemy. | Times you launched fast with imperfect data |
| 5 | **Operate at the lowest level of detail** | Be able to zoom from strategy to the row of data. | Times you debugged a problem nobody else dug into |
| 6 | **And, not either/or** | Reject false dichotomies. Engineer a way to do both. | Times you found a third option in a "must trade off" framing |

## WE ARE LEARNERS

| # | Value | What it means in practice | Story tag idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Truth seek** | Dig in, even when the answer is inconvenient. | Times you surfaced a finding nobody wanted to hear |
| 8 | **1% better every day** | Compounding small improvements over chasing perfection. | Times you iterated weekly on a metric |
| 9 | **Customer-obsessed, not competitor focused** | Center the user. Don't react to competitors. | Times you reframed a comp-driven decision around customers |

## WE ARE ONE TEAM

| # | Value | What it means in practice | Story tag idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Make room at the table** | Diversity + inclusion as a tool for better decisions. | Times you brought in a voice that changed the outcome |
| 11 | **Think outside the room** | Decisions should consider those not present. Decentralized perspective. | Times you considered an absent stakeholder |
| 12 | **One team, one fight** | High accountability, no blame. Wins and losses are shared. | Times you took accountability for a peer's miss |

## How to use this doc

1. When writing a STAR story in [star_story_bank.md](star_story_bank.md), tag it with the **2-3 values** the story most clearly demonstrates.
2. During a behavioral answer, **name the value briefly** if it lands naturally — e.g. "I felt I should be an owner of this even though it wasn't my project." Do not over-name; one or two references max per answer.
3. The behavioral interviewer is a **cross-functional partner** (PM, Eng, Marketing, S&O). Choose stories that involve cross-functional collaboration where possible — that's the lens of the interview.

## Behavioral themes you'll be asked on (from prep kit)

The 4 themes for Part I (Business Partner interview):

1. **Successful partnerships** → stories where you worked with a non-DS team to drive an outcome
2. **Project / deliverable management** → stories about navigating ambiguous scope, shifting priorities, deadline pressure
3. **Leveraging data** → stories where you used data to change a decision (especially against intuition or politics)
4. **Conflict management** → stories where you disagreed with a partner or stakeholder and resolved it

Map each STAR story to at least one of these themes.
