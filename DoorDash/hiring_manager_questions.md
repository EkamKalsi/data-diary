# Hiring Manager Wrap-up — Questions to Ask

Part III is 15 minutes with the hiring manager. The prep kit says: *"have questions prepared."* This is also a signal — the questions you ask reveal how you think about a role, a team, and a business.

> **Aim for 4-5 questions you'd actually ask.** Pick from the categories below, replacing placeholders with your real curiosity. **Do not ask things easily found on the careers page.** Strong questions reveal you've thought about the team's actual work.

---

## Category 1 — Team's current priorities & how this role fits

Pick 1:
- _<e.g. "What's the most important problem the Dasher & Logistics team is trying to crack in the next 6 months, and where does this role sit in that?">_
- _<e.g. "I've read about [specific Dasher-side initiative]. Is the team still investing there, or has the priority shifted?">_
- _<your draft here>_

---

## Category 2 — How decisions get made

Pick 1:
- _<e.g. "When DS recommends something that runs against the consumer growth team's KPIs, how does that conflict get resolved?">_
- _<e.g. "How does the team decide whether to invest in deeper analysis vs ship and learn?">_
- _<your draft here>_

---

## Category 3 — Success in this role

Pick 1:
- _<e.g. "What does success look like for someone in this role in the first 6 months? In year 1?">_
- _<e.g. "When you've hired someone for this team in the past and it didn't work out, what was the most common reason?">_
- _<your draft here>_

---

## Category 4 — The actual work

Pick 1:
- _<e.g. "What's a project the team shipped in the last quarter that you're proud of, and what made it work?">_
- _<e.g. "What's a hard call the team made recently that you'd be curious to debate with me?">_
- _<your draft here>_

---

## Category 5 — Growth & learning

Pick 1:
- _<e.g. "What's the thing this team stretches you on as a leader?">_
- _<e.g. "How do you think about the DS role evolving as DoorDash diversifies beyond food into grocery, retail, drive?">_
- _<your draft here>_

---

## Category 6 — Culture (one short one)

Pick 1:
- _<e.g. "Which of DoorDash's 12 values do you find shows up most strongly on this specific team, and which one is hardest to live?">_
- _<e.g. "What's a ritual or practice on this team that doesn't exist anywhere else at DD?">_
- _<your draft here>_

---

## Tips

- **Start with a question that shows you read about the team's actual work**, not a generic culture question — the hiring manager will remember it.
- **Don't ask "what does the team work on?"** — that's what the recruiter call was for. Ask about how decisions or tradeoffs play out.
- **Leave time for the hiring manager to ask you anything** — they may save a question or two for the end.
- **Don't ask about compensation, equity, or benefits** in this section. Those are for recruiter conversations.
- **One question about a current public DoorDash initiative** signals you follow the company, not just the role.

---

## Closing line (optional)

A short, sincere closing — something like:
> "Thanks for the time today. The thing that landed most for me was [X concrete detail from the conversation]. If you have any reservations about my fit, I'd rather hear them now than after."

This is a high-confidence move; only use it if it lands naturally.
